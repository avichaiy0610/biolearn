## Scientific illustrations — owner-approved workflow
Avichai requires every new scientific illustration and animation to follow [the textbook-informed illustration standard](docs/scientific-illustration-standard.md). Visually inspect the relevant local textbook figure, record the structural conventions, draw an original local prototype outside the website, and show it for review before integration. Preserve scientific content and numeric data when comparing drawing styles; identify proposed content corrections separately. The approved electron-transport-chain illustration is the visual reference for the required quality.
